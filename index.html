The Landis Foundry

Hot metal. Soft paws. Two supervisors.

Choose your character

Weld Bead

    
Hold the button (or SPACE) to heat. Let go to cool. Keep the heat inside the green band to lay the bead. Stay in the red too long and you burn through.

    

    

    
    
  


  

    
The Pour

    
Five pours. Hit the button (or SPACE) when the ladle is over the mold. Each round it gets faster.

    

    

    
    
  


  

    
Midnight Raid

    
You are a raccoon. The cat food is up on a high shelf now, and only the cats can reach it, by jumping from the table. You have to launch from the floor. Press JUMP (or SPACE) when your raccoon reaches the right spot. Each round the shelf moves and the window gets tighter.

    

    

    
    
  


  

    
Shelf Defense

    
Someone is breaking in. You play whoever you picked at the top, up on the shelf with gravity on your side. Press KNOCK (or SPACE) to push the next item off at just the right moment so it lands on the intruder as he walks underneath. Heavy items drop fast, light ones drift. Don't let him reach the safe.

    

    

    
    
  


  

    
Alley Scrap

    
Neighborhood cats keep picking fights behind the foundry. Your opponent shows a tell, and you pick the move that beats it. Swat beats a pounce, Dodge beats a swipe, Hiss beats a stare. Move too early and you flinch. Wait too long and you eat it. Each cat fights differently: Tyrone has the most fluff but the slowest reflexes, Willy is quick but fragile, and Bandit sits in the middle. Keys 1, 2, 3 work too.
